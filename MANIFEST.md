# RISCAN Manifest

## We do not sell computer hardware.

**We sell the confidence that everything will happen exactly as we promised.**

This is the principle on which RISCAN is built.

Technology changes.
Prices change.
Suppliers change.

Trust must never change.

---

## Our Philosophy

Every order is a promise.

Every delivery is proof that we keep it.

We believe that a customer's trust is worth more than a quick profit.

Our goal is not to make a single sale.

Our goal is to earn the next one.

---

## What We Believe

We believe that honesty is a competitive advantage.

We believe that transparency builds trust.

We believe that customers deserve real delivery times, clear communication, and complete respect.

We believe that every customer should always know:

- who they are buying from;
- where their order is;
- how to contact us;
- that their personal data is protected.

---

## Our Responsibility

We are responsible for every order placed through RISCAN.

Responsibility does not end after payment.

It ends only when the customer receives the product and is satisfied with the experience.

---

## Our Standards

- Honest prices.
- Honest delivery estimates.
- Honest communication.
- Secure payments.
- Strong protection of customer data.
- Full shipment tracking.
- Real customer support.
- No hidden surprises.

---

## Our Vision

We are building more than an online store.

We are building a company that people trust.

A company customers want to return to.

Again.

And again.

---

## Our Promise

If a feature increases profit but reduces trust,

**we will not build it.**
